    Pick your operating point

    Every point is one setting of the system, run on the same live stream. Further left means fewer LLM calls; higher means better launch-question accuracy than LLM-only. Bars show the lowest and highest result over seeds. Hover a point for its numbers.

    Default (ringed): static gate + retraining, MiniLM student, alpha_intent 0.03. It is the setting with the fewest LLM calls whose overall accuracy stays within 0.3 points of LLM-only and whose launch-question accuracy stays within 2 points of it, on every one of seeds 0, 1, 2. Settings outside either bar are still shown; hover for overall accuracy and cheap-path errors. The cortisol field is on the curve at one point only and is not the default, so it stays experimental.

    How a query moves

    Scope gateA compiled model decides banking or off-topic, if its doubt is under the bar.
    Intent gateA second compiled model picks one of 77 intents; team and urgency follow from a lookup table.
    EscalateWhen either gate doubts, one LLM call answers and its label joins the training pool.
    AuditA sample of cheap answers is re-checked by the LLM, more often where cortisol is high.
    CortisolMisses and surprises raise the bar globally and locally; new labels retrain the students.
